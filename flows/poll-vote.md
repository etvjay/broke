Open the start page of the poll app.
Create a poll: in the Question box, type Favourite colour?, and in the Options box, type Red on one line and Blue on the next line. Then click Create poll.
Check that the poll "Favourite colour?" appears in the list with "Blue (0)".
Click the Vote button next to Blue.
Check that the message "Vote counted." appears under that poll.
Check that the poll now shows "Blue (1)".
