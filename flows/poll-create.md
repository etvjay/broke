Open the start page of the poll app.
In the Question box, type: Lunch spot?
In the Options box, type Noodles on one line and Tacos on the next line.
Click the Create poll button.
Check that the message "Poll created." appears under the form.
Check that a poll with the question "Lunch spot?" appears in the list.
Check that the poll shows the options "Noodles (0)" and "Tacos (0)".
