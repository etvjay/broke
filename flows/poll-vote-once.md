Open the start page of the poll app.
Create a poll: in the Question box, type Best tea?, and in the Options box, type Green on one line and Black on the next line. Then click Create poll.
Click the Vote button next to Green.
Check that the message "Vote counted." appears under the poll and that the poll shows "Green (1)".
In the same browser, click the Vote button next to Black.
Check that the message "You already voted on this poll." appears under the poll.
Check that the poll still shows "Black (0)", so the second vote did not count.
